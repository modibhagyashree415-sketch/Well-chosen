The Story Behind Well Chosen

Gifting should never feel stressful, generic, or like an afterthought. Yet so often, we find ourselves scrolling endlessly through generic e-commerce listings, unsure if our gift will truly resonate.

Well Chosen was created to solve that dilemma. Developed as an interactive Web Application & Recommendation Platform, Well Chosen bridges human emotion with smart filtering. By pairing an interactive 4-step Gift Finder Quiz with a handpicked catalog of artisanal candles, everlasting botanicals, handcrafted chocolates, and personalized keepsakes, we ensure every gift tells a heartfelt story.

Industrial Training & Project Showcase

Academic Project Overview

This project is designed and implemented by Bhagyashree Modi as part of the industrial training curriculum. The platform showcases modern front-end web architecture, component-based layout design, client-side state management (localStorage cart and wishlist), dynamic multi-factor search algorithms, and responsive mobile-first UI design.

Image Sources & Creative Attribution

In accordance with open creative licensing and fair-use educational portfolio best practices, all photography and aesthetic product visual mockups featured on Well Chosen are carefully attributed to creator communities and platforms:

  • Unsplash & Pexels: High-resolution curated lifestyle photography, botanical bouquets, and cozy pastel interior decor imagery licensed under open creative licenses.
  • Artisan Craft Portfolios: Custom pet watercolor portraits, Kundan Rakhi designs, and floral memory shadow boxes referenced as product conceptualization mockups for educational demonstration.
  • Icons: Font Awesome 6 Free SVG Iconography.
  • Typography: Google Fonts (Outfit, Playfair Display, and Inter).

All images have been cleaned, optimized, and hosted locally in the images/ repository directory with standardized lowercase-hyphenated naming for high-performance GitHub Pages deployment.
